Methodology

How we measure, and how sure we are. Check the work.

Every figure in a Levarum report comes from answers we asked for that week and kept in full. Here is how we ask, how we score, how sure we are and what the numbers can’t tell you.

Draft pending legal review. Details may change before launch.

What we ask, and how

Each brand has 25 buyer questions. We suggest them, and you edit and confirm them. Every Sunday we ask each question three times with web search on, from the brand’s location, because the answer can change from one ask to the next. That gives 75 answers per brand each week. We keep every answer and every source it cites. The report arrives on Monday at 08:30 ET.

Which engine we ask

Today we ask OpenAI’s model ([MODEL NAME]) with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Every report names the engine, model, location and date of the run. We plan to add other engines where their terms allow, and each will be reported separately.

The three scores

Mention rate is the share of the week’s answers that name the brand. In the sample report, 31 of 75 answers do, so the mention rate is 41%. Cited rate is the share of answers that link to the brand’s own site. Share of voice is the brand’s share of all mentions among the brand and the rivals you track. Every score is shown with its count, and by topic: three to six groups of questions you can edit.

Margin of error, and when a change is real

AI answers vary, so every score carries a margin of error. We show it at 95% confidence, calculated from the number of answers counted that week. The three asks of one question aren’t fully independent of each other, so every margin is an estimate. A change since last week has its own, wider margin. We call a change real only when it clears that margin. Otherwise the report says within normal variation.

Question hygiene

A question that names the brand, or drifts away from what a local buyer would ask, makes a score look better than it is. When you edit and confirm your questions, we flag any that name the brand or drift off-topic. If you change a question later, scores from before and after the change don’t measure quite the same thing.

What we don’t claim

A sampled answer is evidence from that run, not a guarantee of future visibility. We don’t promise that a brand will be named or cited, and we don’t claim that a next action caused a change in a score. We measure and suggest. The agency, or the business on Solo, decides what to change. We never contact an agency’s clients about Levarum.

Alerts and trends

Between reports, we send an email when the brand or a tracked rival moves 15 points or more in a week. A 15-point move can still sit inside its margin of error, so check the margin in Monday’s report before you call it a change. From week four, each score also shows a rolling trend across recent weeks.
