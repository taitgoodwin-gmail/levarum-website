Methodology

How we measure, and how sure we are. Check the work.

Every number in a Levarum report can be traced to the answers behind it. This page explains what we ask, what we count, and when a change is real.

What we ask, and how. 75 answers per report.

  1. 25 questions, confirmed by you

    Levarum suggests 25 buyer questions in 3 to 6 topics and flags any that are leading or off-topic. You edit and confirm them. A brand's first report only runs after you do.

  2. Asked three times each

    Each question is asked three times on ChatGPT with web search, through the OpenAI API, from the brand's location. That gives 75 answers per report. The asks are made fresh for every report.

  3. Everything kept

    Every answer and every source it cites is stored with the report, cleaned for safe display, and searchable. Nothing is sampled away.

  4. On demand, or scheduled

    Reports run when you click Run now or on an optional schedule you set. Each plan includes four reports per brand a month. An email goes out once per report when it is ready.

Which engine we ask. Named on every report.

At launch Levarum measures ChatGPT: OpenAI's gpt-5.4-nano with live web search, called through the Responses API. Each ask is made from the brand's location so the engine searches as a local buyer would.

Results through the API can differ from what one person sees in the ChatGPT app on one day. That is one reason every question is asked three times, and why each report names the engine, model, location and date it ran on.

Google Gemini, Perplexity and Claude are rolling out next, on the same 25 questions and the same scoring. We never claim a result for an engine we have not asked.

The three scores. Each with its count.

A score is a share of the 75 answers. Each is shown with the count behind it, its 95% margin of error, and a breakdown by topic.

ScoreWhat it countsSample report
Mention rateThe share of answers that name the brand, by its name or a confirmed alias.31 of 75 answers name Northfield Coffee: 41%, ±11.
Cited rateThe share of answers that cite the brand's own website as a source.12 of 75 answers link to northfieldcoffee.com: 16%, ±8.
Share of voiceThe brand's share of all mentions among the brand and the rivals you track. Rivals are matched by name and website the same way.Northfield holds 26% of 118 mentions across six brands, ±9.

Topics are groups of three to six questions. You can edit them, and each score is also shown per topic so you can see where a rival is named instead.

Margin of error, and when a change is real. No wins by rounding.

  • Every score carries a 95% margin

    The margin is computed from the number of answers counted in the report. With 75 answers a share near 40% carries about ±11 points. Fewer answers, wider margin.

  • It is an estimate

    The three asks of one question are not fully independent, so every margin is an estimate and the report says so.

  • A rival inside your margin is not behind you

    When a rival's score sits inside your margin, the report says the two are too close to call rather than ranking them.

  • Per-report, at launch

    Each report stands on its own. Change between reports, with its own wider margin, and trend lines follow after launch.

Question hygiene

Suggested questions are checked for leading phrasing ("Why is Northfield the best…"), for naming the brand, and for being off-topic for the brand's category. Flagged questions are shown with the reason. You decide what stays.

Matching brands and rivals

A brand is matched in an answer by its name and the aliases you confirm. A citation is matched to the brand's website domain. Rivals are matched the same way, so share of voice compares like with like.

Summary and next actions

The summary (about 120 words) and 3 to 6 next actions are drafted by the model from this report's answers only. Every number in them is checked against the scores before the report is shown.

What we don't claim. Read this too.

We do not claim to know what one ChatGPT user saw on one day. We measure a pattern across 75 fresh answers from a stated location.

We do not claim an engine we have not asked. Gemini, Perplexity and Claude are not in a report until they are.

We do not claim a score moved unless it moved more than its margin. Until change-between-reports ships, each report is read on its own.

We do not promise rankings, traffic or revenue. We show you whether you are in the answer, and give you the evidence.
