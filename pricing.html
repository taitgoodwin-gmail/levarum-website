Questions

Questions before you start.

What is a brand workspace?

One brand or location you track, with its own questions, rivals and weekly report. Agency plans hold several, and you switch between them with one login.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move between Solo, Agency 5 and Agency 15 from your billing page. If you add a brand beyond your plan’s limit, we offer the next plan up.

What happens if I cancel or a payment fails?

Weekly runs stop when the subscription ends or a payment fails. Update your card from the billing page to start them again.

Which AI engines do you measure?

Today, OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Other engines come later, where their terms allow, and will be reported separately. How we measure

Who can see our data?

Only people signed in to your account can see your reports. We don’t sell or rent your data or your clients’ data. To run the service, Stripe handles payment, OpenAI answers the questions and drafts each report’s summary and next actions, and Amazon Web Services hosts it. The Privacy policy lists each one.

Levarum is coined from the Latin levāre: to raise. It’s the root behind relevant and elevate. When someone asks an AI a question, we show you which brands the answer raises, and whether yours is one of them.
