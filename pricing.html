Questions before you start.

What is a brand workspace?

One brand or location you track, with its own questions, rivals and report. Agency plans hold several, and you switch between them with one login.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move between Solo, Agency 5 and Agency 15 from your billing page. If you add a brand beyond your plan’s limit, we offer the next plan up. When you move to a smaller plan, you choose which brands stay active; the rest pause at the end of your billing period, and their past reports stay readable.

What if I cancel or a payment fails?

Cancelling stops renewal at the end of the month you have paid for, and there are no refunds for part months. If a payment fails, Stripe retries the card for 7 days; if it still fails, new reports stop until you update your card from the billing page. Your past reports stay readable.

Which AI engines do you measure?

Today, OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Other engines come later as add-ons, where their terms allow, and will be reported separately. How we measure

Who can see our data?

Only people signed in to your account can see your reports. We don’t sell or rent your data or your clients’ data. Stripe handles payment, OpenAI answers the questions and drafts each report’s summary and next actions, and Amazon Web Services hosts the service. The Privacy policy lists each one.

Levarum is coined from the Latin levāre: to raise. It’s the root behind relevant and elevate. When someone asks an AI a question, we show you which brands the answer raises, and whether yours is one of them.
