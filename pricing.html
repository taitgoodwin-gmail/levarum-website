Plan questions. Answered.

What is a brand workspace?

One brand or location you track, with its own questions, rivals and reports. Agency plans hold several, and you switch between them with one login.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, from your billing page. If you add a brand beyond your limit, you are offered the next plan up. When you move to a smaller plan you choose which brands stay active; the rest pause at the end of the billing period and their past reports stay readable.

What happens if I cancel, or a payment fails?

Cancelling stops renewal at the end of the paid month; there are no refunds for partial months. All your past reports stay readable and exportable. If a payment fails, Stripe retries the card for 7 days; if it still fails, new reports pause until you update your card.

Do unused reports carry over?

No. Included reports reset on your Stripe billing date. Extra reports you buy never expire while you subscribe.

Who can see our data?

Only people signed in to your account. Levarum does not sell or rent your data or your clients' data. Stripe handles payments, OpenAI answers the questions and drafts the summary and actions, and Amazon Web Services hosts the service.
