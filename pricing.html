Questions

Questions before you start.

What is a brand workspace?

One brand or location you track, with its own questions, rivals and report. Agency plans hold several, and you switch between them with one login.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move between Solo, Agency 5 and Agency 15 from your billing page. If you add a brand beyond your plan’s limit, we offer the next plan up. To move to a smaller plan, first archive brands down to its limit.

What happens if I cancel or a payment fails?

Cancelling stops renewal at the end of the month you have paid for, and there are no refunds for part months. If a payment fails, Stripe retries the card for 7 days; if it still fails, daily runs stop until you update your card from the billing page. Your past reports stay readable.

Which AI engines do you measure?

Today, OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Other engines come later as add-ons, where their terms allow, and will be reported separately. How we measure

Who can see our data?

Only people signed in to your account can see your reports. We don’t sell or rent your data or your clients’ data. To run the service, Stripe handles payment, OpenAI answers the questions and drafts each report’s summary and next actions, and Amazon Web Services hosts it. The Privacy policy lists each one.
