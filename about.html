Relevant, by design.

Levarum shows whether a business is raised into an AI answer because it bears on the question. The name is the method.

The name. One verb, four words.

The old words don't fit. "Search" describes ten blue links. "Optimization" describes a ranking. Neither describes what happens when someone asks an assistant a question and gets one answer.

So we went to the root. The Latin levāre means to raise. It gives English four words: lever, elevate, alleviate and relevant. Relevant arrived through the Scottish courts in the 1500s, where it meant evidence that bears on the matter at hand.

That is what an AI assistant does, one question at a time: it raises the few sources that bear on the question and builds its answer from them. If your business is not among them, the answer goes to a competitor.

Levarum is a coined word for showing whether a business is raised in AI answers because it is relevant to the question. The mark is a lever on a fulcrum.
