Questions before you choose a plan.

What is Levarum?

Levarum measures AI visibility on demand. For each report we ask OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API, the questions a brand’s buyers ask, three times each. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Each report shows how often answers name and cite the brand against its local rivals, and what to do next.

Who is it for?

Marketing agencies of any size that serve local and small businesses. On Agency 5 and Agency 15, each report carries your agency’s name. A business without an agency can use Solo: one brand, $79 a month, with the Levarum name on the report.

Which AI engines does it measure?

Today, OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Other engines come later as add-ons, where their terms allow, and will be reported separately.

What does the margin of error mean?

AI answers change between asks, so every score carries a margin of error. A change counts as real only when it clears that margin. Anything smaller is labeled “within normal variation,” so you’re less likely to report a swing as a win. How we measure

How much does it cost?

Solo is $79 a month for one brand, Agency 5 is $249 for five and Agency 15 is $599 for fifteen, in US dollars, billed monthly until you cancel. Each plan includes 4 reports per brand a month, pooled across your account. Extra reports are $9 each, sold 10 for $90. Add-ons per brand: Insights $29 a month, Action drafts $49 a month and Question packs $19 a month for 10 more questions. All prices are provisional until plans open.

How is it different from other AI visibility tools?

Levarum is built around one comparison: each brand against the local rivals you name in its market. The report shows that comparison, the answers and sources behind it, and next actions tied to what it finds.
