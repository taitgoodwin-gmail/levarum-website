How it works

Set it up once. Get a finished report every Monday.

  1. 1Day one

    Add the brand and its local rivals

    Name the brand and 3 to 10 competitors you already watch in its market. After the first week we suggest other brands AI names most often alongside it, so you can swap them in.

  2. 2Day one

    Confirm 25 buyer questions

    We suggest the questions a local buyer would ask. You edit and confirm them. We flag any question that names the brand or drifts off-topic, because those make a score look better than it is.

  3. 3Every Sunday

    We ask, three times over

    Each question goes to OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API, three times, because the answer changes from one ask to the next. We keep every answer and every source.

  4. 4Monday, 08:30 ET

    Your report arrives

    One email with a link to the web report. It reads well on a phone, has a print layout and exports to CSV.

What you get, every Monday

Each brand against its own local rivals. Read the evidence.

What the report tells you, in the order you read it. The answer comes first, then the evidence behind it: every score, every answer and every source.

Summary
One paragraph: where the brand stands, the gap to the leading local rival and what matters most. Every figure in it comes from that week’s answers.
Next actions
Three to six things to do, each tied to the finding behind it.
Three scores
Mention rate: how many answers name the brand. Cited rate: how many link to its own site. Share of voice: its share of all mentions among the brand and the rivals you track.
Change you can defend
Each score shows its change since last week and the margin of error, labeled “real change” or “within normal variation.” From week four, each score also shows a rolling trend.
Topics
Questions grouped into three to six topics you can edit, with the weakest highlighted, so you know where to act first.
Every answer
All 75 answers in full and searchable, with the sources each one cited as links you can open.
Alerts between reports
An email when the brand or a tracked rival moves 15 points or more in a week.

What we stand for

Three things we refuse to do. Trust the evidence.

We won’t call a swing a win.

We call a change real only when it clears its margin of error. Otherwise the report says it is within normal variation.

We won’t claim an engine we haven’t asked.

Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Today that is OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Other engines come later, where their terms allow, and are reported separately.

We won’t step between you and your client.

We measure and suggest. You decide what to change, and the client relationship stays yours.

Questions

Questions before you choose a plan.

What is Levarum?

Levarum is a weekly AI visibility report. Every Sunday it asks OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API, the questions a brand’s buyers ask. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. On Monday you get a report: how often answers name and cite the brand against its local rivals, and what to do next.

Who is Levarum for?

Marketing agencies of any size that serve local and small businesses. On Agency 5 and Agency 15, each report carries your agency’s name, not ours. A business without an agency can use Solo: one brand, $79 a month, with the Levarum name on the report.

Which AI engines does Levarum measure?

Today, OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees. Every report names the engine, model, location and date. Other engines come later, where their terms allow, and will be reported separately.

What does the margin of error mean?

AI answers change between asks, so every score carries a margin of error. A change counts as real only when it clears that margin. Anything smaller is labeled “within normal variation,” so you’re less likely to report a swing as a win. How we measure

How much does Levarum cost?

Solo is $79 a month for one brand. Agency 5 is $249 a month for five brands, about $50 a brand. Agency 15 is $599 a month for fifteen brands, about $40 a brand. Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly until you cancel. You can change plans later.

How is Levarum different from other AI visibility tools?

Levarum is built around one comparison: each brand against the local rivals you name in its market. The weekly report shows that comparison, the answers and sources behind it, and next actions tied to what it finds.
