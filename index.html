Questions people ask. Plain answers.

What is Levarum?

A report that shows whether an AI assistant names your brand when people ask the questions your buyers ask, and how often it names a local rival instead. You get three scores with margins of error, a summary, next actions, and every answer and source.

Who is it for?

Individual businesses that want to track their own brand (Solo), and marketing agencies that track several client brands under their own name (Agency 5 and Agency 15). The method is identical on every plan.

Which AI engines do you measure?

At launch, ChatGPT: OpenAI's model with live web search, through the API, from the brand's location. Results can differ from what one ChatGPT user sees on one day, which is why every question is asked three times and every report names the engine, model, location and date. Google Gemini, Perplexity and Claude are rolling out next.

What does the margin of error mean?

Each score is an estimate from 75 answers, so it is shown with a 95% margin of error. If a rival's score sits inside your margin, the report says so instead of calling it a lead. The method is on the methodology page.

What does it cost?

Solo is $79 a month for one brand, Agency 5 is $249 for five, Agency 15 is $599 for fifteen. Each includes four reports per brand a month, pooled. Extra reports are $9 each, sold 10 for $90. Prices are provisional until plans open.

How is this different from rank tracking?

Search rankings list ten links. An AI assistant gives one answer built from a few sources. Levarum measures whether you are in that answer, whether your site is one of its sources, and how much of the conversation you hold against named rivals.
