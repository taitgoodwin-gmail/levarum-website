The service

Each time you ask for a report on a brand, on demand or on your schedule, Levarum asks an AI engine the buyer questions you confirm and sends you a report on how it answers. Each plan includes 4 reports per brand a month, pooled across the account; extra reports are $9 each, sold 10 for $90. Every report names the engine, model, location and date behind each answer. Today that is OpenAI’s model with web search, through the API. Results may differ from what an individual ChatGPT user sees.

Plans and billing

  • Plans are billed monthly by card through Stripe and renew each month until you cancel.
  • US sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.
  • Cancelling stops renewal at the end of the paid month, with no refunds for part months. If a payment fails, Stripe retries the card for 7 days; if it still fails, new reports stop until the card is updated.
